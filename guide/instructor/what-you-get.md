# What instructors get

For: instructor

Your course has a second site and a second set of notebooks made for teaching. They are built from the same pages students use, so they are always in step with the student version. You don't write or edit anything.

## Open it

You write nothing. Open the instructor site your maintainer set up (an address such as `instructor.<course domain>`; ask them for it). Pull requests can have an instructor preview too, in the preview comment.

## What you see

- **Solutions and explanations inline.** Every exercise's solution and every discussion question's explanation is on the page, in a collapsed box. Students see only a *Click for solution* link.
- **Instructor notes.** Authors' teaching notes (timing, common mistakes, what to say) appear in an "Instructor notes" box. Students never see them.
- **Instructor notebooks.** The Colab and Kaggle buttons open notebooks with the solutions filled in as code cells starting `# Solution`, and the explanations as markdown.
- **The same pages as students**, built from the same source: nothing to copy, nothing out of date.

## Good to know

- **Keep the address to yourself.** The instructor site is not access controlled. It is hidden from search engines (`robots.txt`, `noindex`, no sitemap), but anyone who has the address can open it, and the solutions are also on the public notebooks branch.
- Students use the student site. If you send students a Colab link, take it from the student site's buttons, not the instructor site's: the instructor buttons open notebooks with the answers filled in.
- To add or change teaching notes, ask an author: they go in `{nma-instructor}` ([Instructor notes](../author/instructor-notes.md)).
